# Evidence and claim boundaries

Reviewed 26 October 2026. Links are primary research or the platform provider's own description. Published devices and demonstrations are external evidence, not company achievements.

| Source | What it supports | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| [Feldmann et al., Parallel convolutional processing using an integrated photonic tensor core, Nature 2021](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-020-03070-1) | Soliton-microcomb optical multiply–accumulate computation | Does not establish our process, system cost or advantage |
| [Meyer et al., Deep neural network inference on an integrated, reconfigurable photonic tensor processor, Nature Communications 2026](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-71599-2) | A close architectural baseline and practical precision/readout tradeoffs; Enlightra participates in the research | A source supplier can collaborate on processors; that does not establish a standalone processor product in its catalogue |
| [Hua et al., An integrated large-scale photonic accelerator with ultralow latency, Nature 2025](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-08786-6) | Larger optical arithmetic with integrated electronic support | Reported compute-cycle timing is not a universal host-to-result latency |
| [Zhou et al., In-memory photonic dot-product engine with electrically programmable weight banks, Nature Communications 2023](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-023-38473-x) | Electrically programmed nonvolatile photonic weights | Memory-cell resolution is not complete-processor numerical accuracy |
| [imec/Ghent University integrated silicon-photonics platform example](https://www.imec-int.com/en/press/imec-and-ghent-university-present-fully-integrated-single-chip-microwave-photonics-system) | An example platform with modulators, filters, detectors and tuning | Availability, pricing and suitability for this compute design require direct confirmation |
| [Helgason et al., Power-efficient soliton microcombs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.09410) | Engineered soliton conversion efficiency can improve | Chip optical conversion efficiency is not packaged electrical efficiency |
| [NVIDIA GPU performance background](https://docs.nvidia.com/deeplearning/performance/dl-performance-gpu-background/) | Matrix engines, memory hierarchy and workload-dependent performance | A small optical kernel should not be compared with an unnecessarily inefficient electronic workflow |
