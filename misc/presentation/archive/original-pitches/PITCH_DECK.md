### **Slide 5: Application 1 (Short-Term): The Soliton Accelerator**

**Our first product is a practical, near-term bandwidth accelerator.**

*   **The Concept:** A **Soliton** is a self-reinforcing light pulse that holds its shape, acting as a perfect, indestructible data packet.
*   **The Product:** An on-chip optical interconnect for **ultra-fast inter-core communication**. By replacing electrical wires with light, we can break the bandwidth bottleneck in multi-core processors.
*   **The Impact:** Drastically reduces latency and power consumption. A key goal for this phase is integrating **on-chip light sources** to further improve efficiency.

---

### **Slide 6: Application 2 (Long-Term): The Hopfion Compute Module**

**The next evolution of our platform enables a revolutionary leap into non-binary, analog computing.**

*   **The Concept:** A **Hopfion** is a stable, three-dimensional knot of light. Its complex structure allows for multi-level information storage.
*   **The Product:** An analog compute module that a CPU can call upon for specialized, high-density calculations.
*   **The Impact:** Moves beyond binary to a multi-level, analog paradigm. The hardware for this phase is also ideal for **Reservoir Computing**, a parallel R&D track that leverages the same chip architecture.
