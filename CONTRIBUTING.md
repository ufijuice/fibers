# How to Contribute

We welcome contributions to this project! Thank you for your interest.

## Getting Started
1.  Fork the repository and create your branch from `main`.
2.  Make your changes.
3.  Submit a pull request.

## Contributor License Agreement (CLA)
Before we can accept your contribution, we need you to sign our Contributor License Agreement (CLA).

**Why do we need this?**
The CLA is a simple agreement that protects both you and the project. It clarifies that you are the original author of your work and that you grant the project a license to use your contribution. This gives us the legal certainty needed to manage the project. You still retain full ownership of your copyright.

**How to sign?**
When you submit your first pull request, a bot will automatically comment with a link to sign the CLA. It's a quick, one-time process.

You can read the full text of the agreement here: [Contributor License Agreement](./CLA.md)
