   First Customers and Commercial Path
   Initial customer hypothesis
   Hardware teams with a recurring dense inference layer and a demanding response-time or energy
   budget. First discussions should establish the task, interface, accuracy and purchasing decision.
     1  Specify: agree a representative workload and electronic baseline with a potential evaluator.
     2  Demonstrate: test a packaged compute tile and its software interface.
     3  Evaluate: obtain outside measurements and a defined next integration decision.
     4  Productize: supply compute modules; pursue chiplet or IP integration when justified by
        demand.

  Appendix: Measure the Complete Computation
      Digital vector buffer to digital result buffer, with converter and receiver costs included.
      Measure total electrical draw; separate pump, tuning, conversion, readout and control
      contributions.
      Report host transfer time separately, then include it in the application comparison.
      State weight-update time, input/output rate, calibration duty and operating conditions.
  Arithmetic throughput is only one metric
  For an N-input, M-output tile at r vectors/s:
  NMr MAC/s, or 2NMr operations/s when one MAC counts as two operations.
  An8×8tileatahypothetical 1 Gvector/s gives 0.128 TOPS gross. This does not specify
  precision, total power or application speed.
  Model larger arrays using measured loss, noise, tuning and electrical overhead; do not extrapolate from passive optical
  arithmetic alone.

   Appendix: Process and Integration Choices
        Source: prefer an accessible SiN soliton source with a measured spectrum and power budget.
        Compute: aqualified SOI process with modulators, detectors, filters and test structures.
        Packaging: source and compute dies can remain separate in Gen 1; coupling loss and
        thermal conditions enter the error budget.
        Weights: tunable elements first. Nonvolatile phase-change weights are a later option if
        programming, precision and endurance justify them.
        Alternatives: assess AlGaAs or other platforms when a validated process and team access
        offer an actual advantage.
   Therepository’s generic-PDK ring and retina layouts are historical experiments. A foundry-ready compute layout has not
   been established.
   Source: Example silicon-photonics platform; exact access and device suitability require foundry confirmation
